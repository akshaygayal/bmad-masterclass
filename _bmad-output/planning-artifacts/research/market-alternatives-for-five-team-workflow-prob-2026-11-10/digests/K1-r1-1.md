## Substitute map
- **Dedicated products (inside the tracker ecosystem):** Jira Marketplace DoR/DoD checklist and gate apps such as HeroCoders Checklists, Definition of Done & Ready, ReDo, Checklist Flow and Smart Checklist [3]. These enforce that a checklist is filled in. They do not check that people understand the story the same way (Inference: based on the capability descriptions in [3]).
- **Platform/embedded features:** the Atlassian Rovo Readiness Checker agent, which uses AI to score issue completeness and clarity and can be gated through Jira Automation [1][2]. It scores the text of the ticket, not what each person takes it to mean (Inference: based on the criteria listed in [1]).
- **Non-software practices:** Three Amigos, Example Mapping and BDD/specification by example [4][6][7]. The only role-comparison practice found is Visual Paradigm's role-swap story writing [8]. Evidence that these work is limited to originator claims and self-report surveys [5][7].
- **Services:** none found in this run. Agile coaching and BDD training workshops (e.g. Cucumber webinars, a Ministry of Testing masterclass that appeared in search results) are likely candidates but were not checked.
- **Do-nothing:** clarify during development or testing and absorb the rework. NaPiRE suggests this is common, but the specific effects are unverified [9][10].
