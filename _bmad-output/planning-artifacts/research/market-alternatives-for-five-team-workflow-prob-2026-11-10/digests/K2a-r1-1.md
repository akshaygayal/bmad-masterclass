## Substitute map
- **Dedicated products:** Performance/talent suites with career-track / competency-framework modules (Lattice Grow — claims 8–10). Calibration-focused tools (Confirm — claim 11, vendor claim only). Category inference: "career framework / competency management" modules sit alongside review-cycle tooling; other vendors (e.g., Leapsome, Culture Amp, Pave, Progression) were not verified this run.
- **Platform/embedded features:** Template competency libraries inside HR suites (claims 8–9). No HRIS-native (Workday, BambooHR) evidence retrieved this run.
- **Non-software practices:** Published engineering ladders/rubrics in spreadsheets, GitHub, internal wikis (claims 1–4); manager-set per-report rating expectations reviewed quarterly and separate promo/calibration committees (claim 13); continuous feedback / "no surprises" norms (claim 6); brag docs / promo packets (claim 14, snippet).
- **Services:** Gallup-style engagement measurement and manager coaching (claims 5–6); training firms (Crucial Learning, claim 7). Career-coaching platforms for engineers (Taro, claim 13).
- **Do-nothing:** Annual review with ad-hoc manager judgement; Gallup data (claim 5) suggests about half of employees operate without strongly clear expectations — the default state.

## Looked for but not found
- Independent (non-vendor) measured evidence that transparent ladders or calibration reduce rating surprises or disputes in software organisations. Queries: "performance calibration bias study gender ratings"; source types: search results for academic papers, vendor pages. Only rating-scale design work (claim 12) surfaced, in a teaching context.
- Recent (≤24 months) independent survey specifically on engineers' understanding of meets-vs-exceeds or promotion criteria. Queries: "performance review surprise unclear expectations survey", "engineer promotion moving goalposts calibration". Results were vendor/consultancy surveys or 2015 Gallup data.
- Public reports attributing rating disputes to forced distribution or budget caps vs unclear criteria — no source in this run separated these causes. Same queries as above; Taro thread listings were not visible on the fetched page.
- Primary Lattice docs on whether tracks link to review ratings — not stated in the article read.
