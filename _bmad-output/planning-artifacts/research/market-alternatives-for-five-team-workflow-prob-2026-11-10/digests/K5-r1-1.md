## Substitute map
- **Dedicated products:**
  - AI codebase-understanding and onboarding assistants: Swimm (#12), Zencoder (#14), many hackathon-stage repo-onboarding tools (#15).
  - Unverified this run (named from prior knowledge, needs checking): Sourcegraph/Cody, DeepWiki, Greptile, GitKraken (ex-CodeSee).
  - Generic onboarding-checklist software: Process Street templates appeared in results but were not read.
- **Platform/embedded features:** IDE-embedded AI assistants such as Amazon Q Developer (#13). GitHub Copilot, Cursor and similar tools were not verified this run.
- **Non-software practices:**
  - Mentors and onboarding buddies (#6, #9).
  - Graded task assignment: Simple-Complex, Priority-First, Exploration-Based (#2).
  - Pair programming (#2).
  - Big-picture task briefings (#5).
  - Frequent one-on-ones (#6).
  - Onboarding documentation and checklists (#6, #16).
  - First-commit/first-PR milestones and 30/60/90 check-ins (#16).
  - Informal independence checkpoint (#4).
- **Services:**
  - Transition/handover via shadowing → reverse shadowing → sign-off, offered by GCC/BOT and outsourcing providers such as ANSR (#10, #11).
  - Staffing vendors' onboarding playbooks (#16).
- **Do-nothing:** Learning on the job through ordinary backlog work, with readiness judged by the manager's gut feel. This is the default implied by #3 and #4 (months of low productivity, no formal criteria).

## Leads worth chasing
- Ju et al. full PDF (arXiv 2103.05055): the manager recommendations and the survey instrument, as a basis for readiness criteria.
