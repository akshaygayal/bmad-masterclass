## Pass 1 claims: confirmed / corrected / weakened

- **(a) Ju et al. ICSE 2021, "6+ months, informal independence checkpoint"**: **CORRECTED/WEAKENED in part.** The paper exists and the independence checkpoint is confirmed ("Ten managers considered independence as a final checkpoint"). But "over six months" is a figure the authors **cite from prior work**, not one they measured. The study's own six-month figure is one manager's expectation. It is a single-company study, so it does not establish a cross-organisation pattern. No independent study reporting a comparable manager-defined independence checkpoint was found. The closest independent support for long ramp-up is Krancher's 35-study review (months to years for domain knowledge in transitions, abstract-level).
- **(b) Reverse shadowing with sign-off thresholds**: **NOT CONFIRMED independently.** Only the vendor source (Trupeer glossary) surfaced again. It stays at vendor-claim level.

## Substitute map

- **Informal manager judgement of "independence"** (Ju et al.). This is the current default substitute: unrecorded and subjective.
- **Graduated starter tasks** (Simple-Complex strategy; good first issues in OSS). These are used to train newcomers, not to record readiness.
- **Scaffolded learning tasks plus feedback in outsourcing transitions** (Krancher). This is the closest structural analogue to demonstrated readiness. Verification details were not retrieved.
- **Reverse shadowing / parallel run with sign-off** (vendor-described, BPO/outsourcing).
- **Documentation/tour tools with progress tracking** (Swimm Playlists, GitKraken Code Tours, DeepWiki). These track consumption, not demonstrated ability.
- **Comprehension quizzes** (LACY research prototype; one gamified quiz in the SLR). These test recall or understanding, not task performance.
- **Ramp-up metrics** (time to first or 10th PR, time to first check-in). These are aggregate proxies, noisy for individuals.
- **AI codebase-onboarding skills/agents producing readiness reports and starter tasks** (hackathon/community). These are closest in design and unproven.
