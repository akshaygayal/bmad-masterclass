# K5 r3 check: competitors outside code understanding (skills assessment, competency, LMS sign-off)

Accessed 2026-11-10. Scope: does any established product outside the code-understanding category document the K5 workflow (lead-defined representative task for an existing employee on an unfamiliar project, then attempt, then human record of independent vs assisted work, then specific knowledge gaps, then a human readiness decision)?

Workflow steps: S1 lead defines a representative task in the employee's own project context · S2 employee attempts it · S3 human records evidence of what was done independently vs with help · S4 specific technical/product/business knowledge gaps identified · S5 output informs a human readiness decision.

## Closest products and non-software alternatives

1. **Mitti / SafetyCulture Training practical assessment.** Closest match in workflow shape. A manager/assessor watches an existing team member do a real task and records Complete/Incomplete plus feedback (S1–S2 documented, S3 partial, S5 partial). It is generic, not tied to a project or codebase. No structured independent-vs-assisted field or gap capture was found.
2. **Docebo (and other LMS) observation checklists.** Same pattern, manager-completed checklists for on-the-job application. Vendor claims only (help article was 403). Users report weak reporting.
3. **HackerRank Code Repository questions.** Closest on S1 "own codebase": you can upload your own repo and write custom tasks. Framed for interviews, scored by tests. Use for existing employees, human independent/assisted evidence and gap capture: Unresolved.
4. **CodeSignal Talent Mobility.** Closest on positioning (existing employees, gap analysis, mobility decisions). Rubric/simulation scoring against role profiles, not knowledge of a specific project. Marketing only.
5. **Structured OJT (Jacobs) and skills-matrix manager ratings (non-software).** Closest established practices. S-OJT supplies task-based sign-off. Skills matrices supply gap tracking but rate skills without task evidence.

Implication for K5's differentiation claim (Inference, basis claims 1–14): the separate parts already exist in established products. What was not found is the combination: a task in the employee's own project context, plus structured independent-vs-assisted evidence, plus project-specific technical/product/business gap capture, for internal transfer. The claim survives only as a narrow "combination plus context" claim, at medium-low confidence given the products left unchecked.
