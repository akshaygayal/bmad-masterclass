## Substitute map

- **Dedicated products:** interview-intelligence or notetaker tools that draft scorecards from transcripts, e.g. Metaview [5]. Other names surfaced only in search listings: BrightHire not checked, Kula, HackerRank "Interview Scorecards" KB, Karat (interview-as-a-service). Not verified this run.
- **Platform/embedded features:** ATS interview kits and scorecards with focus attributes, required submission, hidden peer scorecards and reminders, e.g. Greenhouse [1, 2, 4]. The docs show no evidence-quality checking [3].
- **Non-software practices:** structured interviews with predefined questions and anchored rubrics, plus work-sample tests. Research supports these: structured interviews have the highest mean validity, and work samples are moderate [6–8]. Other practices: independent scoring before debrief (GitLab handbook snippet only, not read) and rubric calibration (LeadDev snippet only).
- **Services:** outsourced technical interviewing (Karat appeared in results; not investigated) and I-O psychology consultancies (e.g. HumRRO as authors [6]; their services were not investigated).
- **Do-nothing:** free-text impressions in the ATS, with the feedback antipatterns that persist [12, 13].
- **Regulatory constraints on any recording or assessment tool:** NYC LL144 [9], EU AI Act Annex III [10, 11]. GDPR was not retrieved.
